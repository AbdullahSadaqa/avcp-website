01 Quality Control
QUALITY CONTROL

Reliable Analysis. Controlled Quality.

The Quality Control Department plays a vital role in ensuring that materials and products comply with approved specifications and established quality requirements.

The department provides reliable analytical results supporting the evaluation and release of raw materials, packaging materials, in-process samples, finished products and stability samples.

QC

Quality decisions are supported through AVCP's Quality Circle involving General Management, QC, QA, RA and R&D.

CORE RESPONSIBILITIES

Quality Control Activities

01

Materials & Finished Products

Testing and approval or rejection of raw materials, packaging materials and finished products.

02

In-Process Control

Conducting quality control tests during different stages of manufacturing.

03

Stability Studies

Testing and monitoring stability samples according to approved requirements.

04

Analytical Methods

Analytical method development, verification and validation.

05

OOS & OOT Investigations

Investigation of Out-of-Specification, Out-of-Trend results and laboratory deviations.

06

Certificates of Analysis

Preparation and review of Certificates of Analysis.

07

Laboratory Instruments

Calibration, qualification and maintenance of laboratory instruments.

08

Environmental Monitoring

Environmental and microbiological monitoring where applicable.

09

WFI & RO Systems

Monitoring, testing and reviewing WFI and RO water system data.

WAREHOUSE

Controlled Storage. Full Traceability.

The Warehouse Department is an essential part of AVCP's operational chain, responsible for receiving, storing and monitoring materials required for production.

Materials are managed through controlled procedures designed to maintain quality, appropriate storage conditions and full traceability.

Q

Incoming materials remain in designated quarantine areas until testing is completed and laboratory approval is obtained.

ENGINEERING & MAINTENANCE

Reliable Systems. Continuous Operation.

The Engineering & Maintenance Department supports the safe, reliable, and efficient operation of AVCP's facilities, utilities, production machinery, and packaging equipment. The department works through preventive and corrective maintenance programs, continuous equipment monitoring, and coordination with Production, Quality, and other departments to reduce downtime, maintain operational readiness, and support uninterrupted production.

03

The department focuses on equipment reliability, reduced downtime, and continuous support for production.

CORE RESPONSIBILITIES

Engineering & Maintenance Activities

01

Preventive & Corrective Maintenance

Planning scheduled maintenance and responding to technical breakdowns.

02

Equipment & Utilities

Following up on production, packaging, utility, and supporting equipment.

03

Performance Monitoring

Identifying potential technical issues before they affect operations.

04

Department Coordination

Coordinating maintenance activities with Production, Quality, and related departments.

05

Spare Parts & Technical Support

Following up on spare parts, maintenance materials, and technical requirements.

06

Specialized Services

Supervising external maintenance, inspection, calibration, and technical services when required.

OUR APPROACH

Plan. Respond. Follow Up.

Maintenance activities are organized according to preventive plans, production schedules, equipment requirements, and operational priorities.
